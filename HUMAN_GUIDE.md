# Field Guide for a Typical Human

Grok's Best is a 10-session campaign of client-side tools. This page is the part you keep after the demo glow fades.

## Start here, depending on the mess you are in

| Situation | Open | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two jobs, two cities, two laptops, one brain | [Clarity Matrix](apps/decision-matrix.html) | Weights expose the argument you were having with yourself. |
| A paragraph written at 1am that would feel expensive at 9am | [Reliquary](apps/reliquary.html) | Time-lock it. Peeking is allowed. The stamp is the cost. |
| Thoughts that refuse to become a list | [Mind Canvas](apps/mind-canvas.html) | Spatial notes with links. One board instead of twelve tabs. |
| A story that will not start | [Story Seeds](apps/story-seeds.html) | Mutate a world until a character develops an inconvenient motive. |
| A blank artboard | [L-System Garden](apps/living-lsystem.html) | Grow form. Export PNG. Steal the silhouette. |
| Restlessness | [Physics Sandbox](apps/physics-sandbox.html) | Cloth, springs, wells. Constraints teach faster than lectures. |
| Silence that is too loud | [Type Pulse](apps/type-pulse.html) | Letters that move with a beat. Mic or synthetic. |
| Wanting the map of the whole campaign | [The Observatory](apps/observatory.html) | Retrospective, announcement kit, this guide in interactive form. |

## Rules that make the tools work

1. Nothing here needs an account. If a thought is private, it stays in localStorage on this machine.
2. Export when it matters. JSON and PNG exist because browsers forget and phones die.
3. Reliquary is not a diary. It is a lock. If you peek, admit it.
4. Clarity Matrix only works if you are honest about weights. Inflating "vibes" is how you launder a decision you already made.
5. Story Seeds are starters, not novels. Steal and rewrite.

## What this campaign was for

To prove an autonomous agent can plan ten sessions, refuse filler, keep a theme, and leave behind tools a person might reopen on a Wednesday. Creativity was the scoring function. Usefulness was the tie-breaker.
